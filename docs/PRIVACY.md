# JY Laundry 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-11-21

## 中文

JY Laundry 不要求注册或登录，也不收集你的姓名、电话、邮箱、位置或通讯录等个人信息。

- **应用读取的数据**：应用只从我们的服务器读取宿舍洗衣机的公开状态（空闲、使用中、剩余时间）和使用记录。这些数据只关于洗衣机，不涉及任何用户。
- **保存在你设备上的数据**：服务器地址、访问令牌和你的设置只保存在你的设备上，不会上传。
- **通知**：提醒是由应用在你的设备上生成的本地通知，我们不发送推送通知，也不收集设备标识。
- **服务器日志**：和大多数网站一样，服务器的网页服务器会记录标准访问日志（IP 地址、时间、请求的地址），只用于保持服务正常运行和排查问题，不会与其他数据关联，也不会分享给第三方。
- **不做跟踪**：应用不包含广告、统计分析或第三方跟踪工具。
- **TestFlight**：通过 TestFlight 测试时，Apple 可能会按照其隐私政策向开发者提供崩溃报告和你主动提交的反馈。

如有问题，请通过 TestFlight 的反馈功能联系我们。

## English

JY Laundry needs no account or sign-in, and does not collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email, location or contacts.

- **Data the app reads:** the app only reads the public status of dormitory laundry machines (free, in use, time left) and their usage history from our server. This data is about the machines, not about any user.
- **Data kept on your device:** the server address, access token and your settings stay on your device and are not uploaded.
- **Notifications:** reminders are local notifications created by the app on your device. We do not send push notifications or collect device identifiers.
- **Server logs:** like most websites, the server's web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, requested address). They are used only to keep the service running and to fix problems, are not combined with other data, and are not shared with third parties.
- **No tracking:** the app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking tools.
- **TestFlight:** when you test through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy.

For questions, contact us through TestFlight's feedback feature.
